Past Shows

  • Wednesday, Sept 24, 2025: "In Through the Locked Door" at the Middle East Corner in Cambridge, MA, with Aaron Gray and host Jim Healey. This was a wonderful time on a dreary rainy day. I had two servings of falafel, very Middle East. I played a 40 min set. I always love taking the commuter rail out to stretch my legs on out of town stages and I'm very grateful this happened.Here's a Google Drive link to a phone recording of the whole set.

  • Sunday, Sept 21, 2025: Amber Lane Cafe and Pub in Northampton, MA. This was a pleasant outdoors afternoon set with positively beautiful weather. Half covers, half originals, with a chill audience who I talked to more than I usually do. Super enjoyable. Very thankful to Ximena and Amber Lane staff for scheduling me (and allowing me to use their easy up to dodge the sun).

  • Saturday, Sept 13, 2025: Element Brewing Company in Millers Falls, MA. A quiet, pleasant afternoon of music playing for a small group of locals. Super grateful for Rick having me play there. See you again sometime soon.

  • Saturday, Sept 13, 2025: Gardner Area League of Artists 25th Anniversary, at noon, at Old Murdock in Winchendon, MA. This was a freaking dream. Old Murdock was built in 1867 or so and has a totally ancient and beautiful performance hall with vaulted ceilings. It's a little rumbly and echoey but I've always dreamt of playing in there. Major thanks to Gardner Area League of Artists and my pal Franco (Soundbites Events) for having me on and running sound. And of course, love to all the artists who came out with their mindblowing work which was so much more impressive than what I had to offer.

  • Saturday, Sept 6, 2025: Bull Run Restaurant, playing in the lounge, 215 Great Rd, Shirley, MA @ 6PM. The Bull Run has been open for like 300 years and is a place I'm happy to entertain in. I played half a 3 hour set of covers, the other half all my own stuff. It was great. And I got a great dinner after too.

  • Wednesday, Sept 3, 2025: Somerville Armory "In The Round" songwriter series @ 7PM. 191 Highland Avenue, Somerville, MA. This was a cool show in a cool place! I played with Kat Ivy, a singer songwriter from up in New Hampshire, Sam Bergquist, a great picker of my kinda stylistic persuasion, and our host, David Thorne Scott, who gave me a ride to the train after, because he's a swell fella. We played inside one of the little parapets in the front of the Armory, and in the main room, Oso Oso was there on tour.
