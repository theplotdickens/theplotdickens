Music

  • 2018 11 08: "POSTERITY MEASURES". Click for the bandcamp link. I recorded this throughout 2017 and 2018 at Loud Sun Studio in Jaffrey, NH. My first full band album, I love this album a lot and I am thankful to everyone involved. Album artwork by Madison Pierce.
