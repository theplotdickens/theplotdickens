Music
(I haven't done albums in a while but they are out there!)

  • 2018 11 08: "POSTERITY MEASURES". Click for the bandcamp link. I recorded this throughout 2017 and 2018 at Loud Sun Studio in Jaffrey, NH. Produced by Ben Rogers with aid from Mia Govoni, drums by Ben Rogers, electric guitars and bass by Ian Galipeau. I am really proud of this record and it is a short, wordy punchy image of where I was at the time. I'm writing a lot different music now. Album cover by Snide Molly.
  • 2016 06 18: "AND THE PLOT DICKENS". Click for the bandcamp link. Recorded in early 2016 at Ian Galipeau's home studio in Keene, NH. Ian Galipeau on drums, bass, electric guitar and banjo. My first full band album, I love this album a lot and I am thankful to everyone involved. Album artwork by Madison Pierce.
